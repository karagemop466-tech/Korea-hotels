# Cheonan — Hotels & Booking Guide (Recommended 3rd City)

**Why Cheonan?**  
Perfect midway stop on the Seoul–Busan KTX corridor. Excellent balance of **lower hotel costs**, **fast direct high-speed rail**, and **convenience** for airport arrivals/departures at Incheon (ICN). Ideal 6-night base when doing Seoul + Busan.

## Train Access & Schedules (2026 estimates)
- **Seoul → Cheonan** (KTX): 30–50 minutes, ~$10–25 one-way. Frequent departures from Seoul Station / Yongsan.
- **Cheonan → Busan** (KTX): 1h 40m – 2h, ~$20–35 one-way. Direct on Gyeongbu Line.
- **Cheonan → Incheon Airport (ICN)**: KTX to Seoul (~40 min) + AREX Airport Railroad (~45 min). Total ~1h 30m–2h door-to-door.
- **Cheonan Station** (downtown) vs **Cheonan-Asan Station** (KTX hub, slightly north): Both well-connected. Cheonan-Asan is the main high-speed stop.

**Tip**: Use the Korail app or Trip.com for tickets. T-money card works on local lines. Book KTX 1–2 weeks ahead for best prices during shoulder season (Oct–Nov).

## Recommended Areas to Stay
- **Near Cheonan Station** (central, walkable to shops/restaurants)
- **Near Cheonan-Asan KTX Station** (best for rail convenience)
- **Buldang / Samsung Display area** (modern hotels, good value)

## Practical Tips
- **Check-in/out**: Most 15:00 / 11:00–12:00.
- **Breakfast**: Many include or offer buffet options (highly rated at Shilla Stay & Ramada).
- **Getting around**: Excellent subway (Line 1) + buses. Taxi to Cheonan-Asan KTX ~10–15 min.
- **Things to do**: Independence Hall of Korea, shopping at Shinsegae/ department stores, local food (hotteok, galbi), easy day trips back to Seoul.
- **Airport flow**: Arrival at ICN → AREX → Seoul Station → KTX to Cheonan (total ~2 hours). Reverse on departure.
- **Shoulder season advantage**: Lower rates + beautiful autumn weather (late Oct–early Nov).

**Cost savings example (6 nights)**: Hotels ~$360–660 total vs $600–900 in Seoul. Transport (Seoul–Cheonan–Busan legs) ~$60–120. Net savings of $200–400+ while maintaining efficiency.

This makes Cheonan the smartest third-city choice for your itinerary!
